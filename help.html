Redeem guides

Our policies

Digital cards: bank transfer only

Digital codes are paid by online or mobile banking (or card, once card payments are live). Your order reference must be in the transfer note.

No refunds once a code is sent

Digital codes are delivered instantly and can't be taken back, so they can't be refunded once they're forwarded to you. Check your account region before you buy.

Gear for delivery: 25% deposit

Physical items are reserved with a 25% non-refundable deposit by bank transfer. Pay the balance by transfer or cash on delivery.

Returns on physical items

Bring the original receipt and the item in the condition it was received. Items damaged by negligence, or reported stolen, can't be refunded.

FAQ

How fast will I get my code?
Most codes are sent within 5 minutes of your slip being uploaded, 8 AM to midnight, every day. Orders placed overnight are sent first thing in the morning.
Which region are the cards?
PSN, Xbox, Nintendo, Apple and Netflix cards are for US accounts, which is what most gamers in T&T use. Roblox, V-Bucks and Razer Gold work globally.
What if my code doesn't work?
Message us on WhatsApp with your order reference and a screenshot of the error. Every code is checked before it's sent, and we'll sort it out right away.
Can I buy a card bigger than US$100?
Yes. Message us on WhatsApp and we'll quote any amount.
Can I send a code as a gift?
Yes. Put the gift receiver's email at checkout, or forward the code from your order page.
